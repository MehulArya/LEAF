# 🍃 Leaf

### Where Ideas Grow Together

**Leaf** is a community collaboration platform built for college students to discover, create, and grow communities around their interests.

Traditional college clubs are often fixed and limited. Leaf gives students the freedom to create communities around **anything they are passionate about**—technology, music, fitness, photography, entrepreneurship, gaming, arts, or completely new ideas.

### 🌱 The Idea Behind Leaf

Every student is a **leaf**—unique, independent, and full of potential.

Communities are the **branches** that bring students with shared interests together.

As students connect, collaborate, propose ideas, and organize activities, these branches grow into a larger **student ecosystem**.

> **Every student has an idea. Leaf gives it a place to grow.**

### 🚀 Core Features

* 🔐 Student authentication & authorization
* 🌿 Create and manage communities
* 🔎 Discover and search communities
* 🏫 Intra-college & inter-college communities
* 👥 Communities with up to 100 members
* 📢 Community information board
* 💬 Community, direct, and admin-support chat
* 💡 Idea proposals with upvotes & downvotes
* 📅 Turn community ideas into upcoming events
* ⭐ Community reviews and ratings
* 🔔 Notifications

### 🏗️ Tech Stack

**Frontend:** React.js
**Backend:** Django + Django REST Framework
**Database:** PostgreSQL
**Real-time:** Django Channels + WebSockets
**Cache / Messaging Layer:** Redis

### 🎯 Vision

Leaf aims to create a healthy and open environment where students can **find their people, share their ideas, collaborate on meaningful activities, and build communities beyond the boundaries of traditional college clubs.**

**Find your people. Grow your ideas. 🍃**
